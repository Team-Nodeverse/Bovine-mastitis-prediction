# 📚 Research & References

CattleΨic is being developed using research related to bovine mastitis, milk-quality parameters, sensor-based monitoring and edge computing.

## Research Areas

- Bovine mastitis detection
- Milk electrical conductivity
- Milk pH variation
- Milk temperature monitoring
- Turbidity and optical milk analysis
- Edge computing in livestock monitoring
- Machine learning for animal-health screening

## Validation Plan

The project will require:

- Labelled healthy and mastitis milk samples
- Veterinary consultation
- Sensor calibration
- Comparison with laboratory results
- Larger field datasets
- ML model evaluation using appropriate validation metrics

## Important Note

Research references and validated datasets will be documented here as the project progresses.

CattleΨic is currently a prototype for early risk screening and does not provide a confirmed veterinary diagnosis.
